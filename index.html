About Me

Hello! I am Abduhoo Bin Nasir, a Computer Science undergraduate at the Institute of Space Technology, Islamabad. I lead game development at DevCore and teach OOP as a Teaching Assistant Intern.

My focus is C++ systems architecture and low-level software design. I build game engines from the ground up, including raycasting renderers with a custom DDA algorithm, and I work on P2P multiplayer networking for real-time games. I like clean class hierarchies, readable code and understanding how things work underneath.
